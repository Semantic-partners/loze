# loze
Lowe Inspired Upper Ontology

A top-level ontology largely inspired - but with a few deviations or additions - by the four-category ontology defended by the philosopher E. J. Lowe and Eddy Zemach.

A peculiar feature is the category of schematic particulars: these are repeatable like classes, but have many of the properties that are typical of ordinary individuals. While schematic particulars might come across as weird to those who are used to class-individual duality, we believe that they can provide surprisingly powerful design patterns, which can be a game-changer in circumstances in which the standard solution would be to use restrictions or punning.
